      8. Append bets to bets_path (use PaperReconciler._atomic_write_parquet pattern).
      9. Mark PREDICTED with bet_ids in RaceProgress, or NO_BET if no bets generated.
      10. On exception -> mark FAILED with failure_reason, append ExitCode.GENERAL_ERROR to errors, continue to next race.
